Frequently asked questions

You deposit crypto into your SPay wallet, then add funds to your card from your USDC balance using quick amounts between $10 and $500. The credit updates in minutes, and the card spends in US dollars.

USDT on Tron (TRC-20) and Ethereum (ERC-20), USDC on Ethereum (ERC-20), native ETH and native TRX. Each token and network combination gets its own deposit address, balance and live USD value.

A teal plastic Visa card is on the way, with in-store taps and ATM withdrawals. Today the card is virtual: created in-app, spendable immediately online and in stores, and addable to Apple Wallet.

Apple Pay is live — add your card to Apple Wallet from the card screen and tap to pay. Google Pay support is in development.

You earn points on every card purchase, at a rate that depends on your tier. Points convert to USDC cashback at 200 points per dollar and can be redeemed from 1,000 points, credited straight to your card balance.

Each tier has a cap for monthly, daily and single-transaction spending, and you set your own limit anywhere from $0 up to that cap. Limits reset automatically. Platinum caps at $20,000 a month, Signature at $50,000 and Infinite at $100,000.

Withdrawals are sent as USDC on the Base network to any self-custody wallet. Confirm your receiving wallet supports Base before sending, because funds sent on the wrong network cannot be recovered.

The card settles merchants in US dollars over the Visa network, so merchants are never paid in crypto. Balances can be displayed in dirhams, and the app is fully localised in Arabic with right-to-left support. Eligibility depends on verification — see Support for current country coverage.
